FOOTBALL TOURNAMENT

RULES & REGULATIONS

Please read the following rules and regulations carefully before participating in the tournament.

REGISTER YOUR TEAM
  1. Registration

    All participating teams must submit their registration forms before the deadline.

  2. Match Duration

    Match duration and format will be decided by the tournament organizers and communicated to all participating teams before the matches.

  3. Substitution

    Substitutions will be allowed as per the tournament rules communicated before the start of the competition.

  4. Reporting Time

    All teams must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match time.

  5. Discipline

    Players, coaches and team officials must maintain discipline and respect opponents, referees and organizers. Misconduct may result in disciplinary action.

  6. Referee's Decision

    The referee's decisions regarding match play must be respected. Any protest or complaint must be submitted to the tournament organizers by the team coach.

  7. Fair Play

    All teams are expected to play fairly and honestly. Unsporting behaviour, deliberate misconduct or violation of tournament rules may lead to penalties or disqualification.

  8. Organizers' Authority

    The tournament organizers reserve the right to resolve disputes, modify the schedule and take necessary decisions regarding matters not covered under these rules.

Important

All participating teams are requested to carefully read and follow the tournament rules and regulations.
