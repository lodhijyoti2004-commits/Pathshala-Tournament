Sports

Most of our children come from the streets, construction sites or slums where education is not a priority. When they first come to us, they often face challenges that can make it difficult for them to concentrate and sit in a classroom for many hours.

One thing that they are naturally good at is sports.

From our early days in 2013, we have always had sports as part of the curriculum. We have hosted Sports Days since then on different public grounds.

What we were lacking was a dedicated space and infrastructure where we could provide formal coaching for our children so that their natural strength and stamina could be channelled into sports performance.

Formal Coaching

Since 2019, we have moved to the Pathshala Hostel Campus grounds with a football field and basketball court on campus.
