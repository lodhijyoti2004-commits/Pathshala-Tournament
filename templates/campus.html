Campus

In September 2019, we moved into the Pathshala Hostel building. Since then, we have continued to receive good support from our well-wishers and have been able to expand our initiatives on campus.

In May 2022, we inaugurated our Informal School classrooms on campus. Our children are now studying on campus.

We added many environmentally friendly additions like solar cookers, rainwater harvesting and a Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) right from the beginning.

Pottery section, online classes, library, Skill Development Center, and Chemistry/Physics lab are some of the facilities available to our children on campus.

The move to the hostel has given our children direct access to our football field and basketball court.

In addition, they have bicycles, Malkhamb, Kabaddi, Kho-Kho and Karate for their physical fitness.
