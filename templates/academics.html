Academics

Hybrid Approach to Learning

Since 2022-2023, we have combined Project Based Learning with conventional education such that the children are able to learn their fundamentals till Grade 7 through hands-on projects that address real-world problems.

Children learn through various mediums of expression like essays, model making, dance, music, theatre, art, etc. The format is selected based on what best suits the child and the topic at hand.

Levels of Learning

We have 5 levels of learning. We have eliminated the concept of grade-based learning till Class 7.

Each learning level is designed keeping the State Board education level expectations for Grade 7 in mind. Projects are chosen so that all relevant topics are covered by the time children reach Level 5, and in much greater detail.

Grade 8 & Beyond

The expectation is that by the time the children finish all 5 levels of learning, their fundamentals will be strong enough to transition to the conventional education system.

While we truly believe in the benefits of Project Based Learning, our ultimate goal is for our children to succeed professionally.

For that, the children will need to take the Class 10 and Class 12 examinations to qualify for different professional courses.

Therefore, starting in Class 8, they follow the OBE (Open Basic Education) curriculum of the National Institute of Open Schooling board.

NIOS is at par with other State and Central Boards and is recognized by colleges and universities.
