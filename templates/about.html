Srotoshwini is a Vadodara city based Trust, registered in 2010, primarily aimed at working towards the development of underprivileged children and women.

The Trust is 12AA, 80G and FCRA compliant. There are 3 main initiatives that run under the Trust.

Pathshala is an informal school that has a hybrid learning program which focuses on creative, child-centred education and overall development. Pathshala Informal School: a school that has a hybrid learning program that marries the guidelines of Reggio Emilia Philosophy of child-centric education (Project Based Learning) with NIOS board learning expectations so the end result is a very skilled individual ready for joining the workforce. Pathshala Hostel: a residential facility for children where the focus is, all round development of the child including academics, sports, extra curricular activities, vocational training & career counseling. Pathshala classes now run on hostel campus.
